ACME CORPORATION — IT SECURITY AND ACCEPTABLE USE GUIDELINES
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: March 15, 2026
=============================================

SECTION 1: PASSWORD POLICY

1.1 Password Requirements
All passwords must meet the following minimum requirements:
- Minimum length: 12 characters
- Must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter,
  one digit, and one special character
- Must not contain the user's name, username, or common dictionary words
- Must not reuse any of the last 10 passwords

1.2 Password Rotation
Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system will prompt users
14 days before expiration. Accounts with expired passwords will be locked
after a 3-day grace period.

1.3 Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is required for:
- All VPN connections
- Access to production systems
- Administrative consoles
- Financial applications

SECTION 2: DATA CLASSIFICATION

2.1 Classification Levels
All company data must be classified according to the following levels:

- PUBLIC: Information intended for public release. No restrictions.
- INTERNAL: General business information. Available to all employees.
- CONFIDENTIAL: Sensitive business data. Access restricted to authorized
  personnel on a need-to-know basis.
- RESTRICTED: Highly sensitive data including PII, financial records,
  and trade secrets. Requires encryption at rest and in transit.

2.2 Handling Requirements
- RESTRICTED data must be encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent.
- CONFIDENTIAL data must not be stored on personal devices.
- All data transfers outside the corporate network require manager approval.

SECTION 3: INCIDENT RESPONSE

3.1 Reporting
All security incidents must be reported to the IT Security team within
1 hour of discovery via the Security Incident Portal or by calling the
24/7 Security Hotline at extension 5555.

3.2 Severity Levels
- CRITICAL: Active data breach or system compromise. Response: Immediate.
- HIGH: Potential breach or vulnerability being actively exploited. Response: 4 hours.
- MEDIUM: Suspicious activity that may indicate a threat. Response: 24 hours.
- LOW: Minor policy violation with no evidence of data exposure. Response: 72 hours.

3.3 Post-Incident Review
A post-incident review must be conducted within 5 business days of incident
resolution. Findings must be documented and shared with the CISO.

SECTION 4: ACCEPTABLE USE

4.1 Company Equipment
Company-provided equipment is for business use. Limited personal use is
permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties or violate
company policies.

4.2 Prohibited Activities
The following activities are strictly prohibited on company networks:
- Unauthorized access attempts to any system
- Installation of unlicensed or unapproved software
- Sharing credentials with any other person
- Bypassing security controls or content filters
- Downloading or distributing copyrighted material

4.3 Monitoring
The company reserves the right to monitor all network traffic, email
communications, and system usage on company-owned equipment. Employees
should have no expectation of privacy when using company resources.
