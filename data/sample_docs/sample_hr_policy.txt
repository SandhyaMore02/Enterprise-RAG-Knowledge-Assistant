ACME CORPORATION — HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY HANDBOOK
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026
=============================================

SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their probationary period of 90 days
are eligible for annual leave benefits.

1.2 Leave Entitlement
- Full-time employees: 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.
- Part-time employees: Annual leave is prorated based on scheduled hours.
- Interns: Interns receive 5 days of annual leave during their internship period.

1.3 Leave Accrual
Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month of continuous service.
Unused leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the
following calendar year. Any excess is forfeited on March 31.

1.4 Leave Request Process
Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 5 business
days in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave may be requested with
same-day notice with manager approval.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY

2.1 Eligibility
Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week after
completing 6 months of employment.

2.2 Equipment
The company provides a laptop and necessary software. Employees are responsible
for maintaining a suitable home office environment and reliable internet
connection with minimum 25 Mbps download speed.

2.3 Availability
Remote workers must be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in
their local time zone. Responses to messages should occur within 30 minutes
during core hours.

SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE REVIEW

3.1 Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually in June and December.

3.2 Rating Scale
Employees are evaluated on a 5-point scale:
- 5: Exceptional performance, consistently exceeds expectations
- 4: Strong performance, frequently exceeds expectations
- 3: Meets expectations, satisfactory performance
- 2: Needs improvement, partially meets expectations
- 1: Unsatisfactory, fails to meet expectations

3.3 Promotion Criteria
Promotion eligibility requires:
- Minimum 18 months in current role
- Two consecutive review periods rated 4 or above
- Completion of required training programs
- Manager and department head recommendation

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT

4.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct and treat
colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity.

4.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager
and the HR department within 30 days of becoming aware of the conflict.

4.3 Confidentiality
Company proprietary information, including client data, financial records,
and strategic plans, must not be shared with unauthorized parties. Violation
may result in immediate termination and legal action.
